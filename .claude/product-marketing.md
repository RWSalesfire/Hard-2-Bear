## What it is

Hard To Bear is a side-business web design agency run by Russell Westgarth. It builds websites for local, owner-run businesses, and it gets them found on Google.

## Who it's for

- Pubs and restaurants, garages and mechanics, driving instructors, barbers and salons, and similar local businesses.
- Owner-run, busy, not technical. Often no website, or one that's broken on a phone.
- Sceptical of agencies, and comparing against Wix at about £15 a month or a cheap freelancer.

## What they actually want

- To be found on Google Maps and in "near me" searches.
- A site that works on a phone, with tap-to-call, opening hours and directions.
- To update menus, prices and hours themselves, or to have someone to ring who'll do it.
- A clear fixed price, no jargon, done in a couple of weeks.

## Why Hard To Bear

- One real person, not a faceless agency.
- Prices are on the website, so there's no quote dance.
- Local search is built in, not sold as an extra.
- The first client is a real local pub, used as the main case study.
